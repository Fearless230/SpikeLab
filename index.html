Your game
Measure your jump now: 2 minutes, a wall and some tape
  1. Stand side-on to a wall, feet flat. Reach up with one arm and mark the highest point. Measure it from the floor: that is your standing reach.
  2. From a standstill, jump with both feet and touch the wall as high as you can. Best of three.
  3. Enter both and your jump is worked out for you.
